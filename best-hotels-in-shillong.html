Why Stay in Shillong Meghalaya

Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya and one of the most popular tourist destinations in Northeast India. Located at an altitude of about 1,496 metres above sea level, the city offers a pleasantly cool climate throughout the year. Even in summer, the temperature stays mild — which is why tourists from hot cities like Delhi, Kolkata, and Mumbai love visiting Shillong.

The city has a lot to offer: beautiful lakes like Umiam Lake, exciting waterfalls like Elephant Falls and Beadon Falls, the buzzing Police Bazar market, Don Bosco Museum, and easy day trips to Cherrapunji and Mawsynram — the wettest places on Earth. Shillong also has a strong music culture and lively food scene that is unique in all of India.

Best time to visit Shillong: October to May offers the most pleasant weather. The monsoon months (June to September) are very rainy but make the hills incredibly lush and green. Hotels in Shillong Meghalaya tend to fill up quickly during peak tourist season, so booking in advance is always a good idea.

Hotels Near Police Bazar Shillong

Police Bazar is the most popular and busy commercial area in Shillong. It is the shopping, dining, and entertainment hub of the city. If you stay near Police Bazar, you can walk to restaurants, shops, cafes, and bus stands very easily. Most tourists prefer to stay in this area because of its central location and easy connectivity to all major attractions.

Pro Tip: Hotels near Police Bazar Shillong tend to book up fast during holidays and tourist season (October to February). Always book at least 2–3 weeks in advance to get the best rates and room availability.

Some of the best hotels near Police Bazar Shillong include Polo Towers, Hotel Indiana, and Hotel Centre Point — all of which are within easy reach of the market area. Staying here means you are always close to food, transport, and all the action that Shillong has to offer.

How to Choose the Best Hotel in Shillong

Choosing the right hotel depends on your personal needs, travel style, and budget. Here are the most important factors to consider when booking a hotel in Shillong:

1. Budget

Decide how much you want to spend per night before you start searching. Shillong has options for every budget — from dorm beds at ₹400 per night at Zostel to premium suites at ₹10,000+ per night at luxury hotels. Setting a clear budget helps you narrow down your options quickly.

2. Location

Police Bazar is the most convenient area to stay in Shillong for first-time visitors. If you prefer a quieter stay, areas like Laitumkhrah or Malki are also good options with easy access to the city center. Always check how far the hotel is from your planned attractions before booking.

3. Amenities

Shillong can get very cold, especially from November to February. Make sure your hotel offers room heaters or fireplaces. Hot water, good Wi-Fi, and clean bathrooms are other important things to check before booking a hotel in Shillong Meghalaya.

4. Tourist Season

Hotel prices in Shillong are higher during peak season (October–February and May–June for summer holidays). If you travel in the off-season, you can get much better deals on the same hotels. Always compare prices on multiple booking platforms before making a final decision.
